Our volunteer based community creates accessible, social workouts in urban centres around the world. We aim to bridge the gap between enjoying your workout and hanging out with your friends. Seasoned runner or just starting out? It doesn't matter to us. We run with speakers and create a party that moves through the streets every week and everyone is invited.
